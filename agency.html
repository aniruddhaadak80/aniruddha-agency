how engagements run

Discover, build, harden, hand over.

step 01 · discover

Scope the agent, not just the app

We map which parts of your workflow deserve autonomy and which deserve plain automation. Agents are expensive; judgment about where they fit is the real service.

step 02 · build

Ship a thin vertical slice fast

A working slice in days, not months: real data, real model calls, real UI. You watch it think before we invest in depth.

step 03 · harden

Fight the failure modes

Timeouts that ignore cancellation signals, agents that freeze mid-plan, silent API aborts — I have debugged these in public repos and wrote about them. Your system gets those scars pre-applied as immunity.

step 04 · hand over

You own everything

Clean repos, documented prompts, evaluation scripts and a handover walkthrough. The goal is that you stop needing me — then come back anyway.
